## 🌐 Deploy in 2 Minutes (Easiest Method)

### Using Vercel (Recommended)

1. **Go to:** https://vercel.com/new

2. **Click:** "Continue with GitHub"

3. **Import:** Select `samiulhassan-stack/internshiptask5`

4. **Click:** "Deploy" (settings are auto-detected)

5. **Wait:** 1-2 minutes

6. **Done!** Copy your live URL 🎉
